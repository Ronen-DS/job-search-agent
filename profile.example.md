# Candidate profile (example)

A fictional candidate showing the format of profile.md. Copy this file to profile.md and replace the content with your own; profile.md is kept out of git.

## My profile
- Degrees: MSc in Epidemiology; BSc in Statistics.
- Recent course: data engineering with Python and SQL.
- Experience: ~8 years, mostly in public-health research and a health-insurance analytics team.
- Core expertise: biostatistics for clinical and population studies (survival analysis, mixed models, causal inference).
- Core expertise: real-world data from electronic health records and claims; cohort building and outcome studies.
- ML: gradient-boosting risk models for hospital readmission. No deep-learning experience.
- Writing: ~6 peer-reviewed papers and several statistical analysis plans.
- Tools: R (main), Python, SQL; SAS (older); Git. Some experience with Azure.

## Target roles
- Biostatistician or Senior Biostatistician.
- Epidemiologist or Real-World Evidence (RWE) Scientist.
- Data Scientist in healthcare or pharma.
- Adjacent roles if lack of direct experience is acceptable: clinical data manager, medical writer for statistical sections.
- Out-of-field roles (optional; to gain experience elsewhere): e.g. general data analyst or data scientist roles in any industry, junior to mid-level only (never senior, lead, head or director).

## Rating rules
- Roles in my field can be rated strong; out-of-field roles "possible" at most, listed after the in-field roles in the digest.
- In gaps, mark each one learnable (a few weeks, e.g. a tool or cloud basics) or core (e.g. years of experience in something).

## Dealbreakers
- Skip pure software engineering roles and roles in fields unrelated to health data.
- Skip roles that require a medical licence.

## Location
- Best: Haifa and the Krayot.
- Also fine: anywhere up to about 60 minutes by train from Haifa.
- Hybrid preferred; fully remote is fine.
